# Departures from the ROM

Every place the game differs from tf_180, why, and what replaces it. Interim rows go away when the ROM
extraction delivers the data.

| Area | ROM | Here | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| Score display (deff 19) | 0x0102fb8c | drawn live from the captured draw calls; the dim level of players not up and the font for scores wider than 87 dots are inferred (tf/score_screen.py) | inferred details |
| Display effects with values | the ROM prints live values (scores, counts, credits, high scores) | the printf texts of 71 effects are cleared from the captured frames and drawn live from their ROM format (scripts/gen_media.py value_texts), fit-font texts in the font the ROM's fit picks (deff_values.gd), with the values the rules pass (pop: 3,170; bonus: count value and total; which RAM value each text prints is inferred); a slot with no value keeps the capture's string, and texts in moving or fading frames stay as captured (the bonus border frames), as do the pages a capture does not hold (bonus 2X and up); the status panel is live | interim, until A gives each text's argument source |
| Deffs without frames (39) | they draw live state or nothing in the capture window | nothing drawn (the previous screen stays) | interim |
| Scoring and rules | switch handlers and the features they call | as the ROM for the handlers, slings, pops, lanes, spinner, Bumblebee, 2-bank, combos, shot multipliers, skill shots, Energon, the Allspark award, mode-start shots and the eight battles (rom/rules/modes/*.md; tf/features/). Missing: the Optimus motor and target, the multiball shot inserts; the roving 3X and the combo arrows (leff 34) are not drawn. Inferred: double scoring's end split, the right orbit ignore windows (3 s after a left orbit pass, the center lane, a plunge or the back door), the battle hit deffs' lengths | interim, until the specs |

| Multiball and wizard deffs without frames | deffs 59, 60, 62 (Optimus Autobot), 79, 139 (Megatron), 88 (wizard multiball) have no capture | lengths, leffs and sounds from the traces (UNCAPTURED in tf/features/optimus.py, megatron.py, wizard.py); nothing drawn | inferred |
| Multiball intro timing | the Optimus intro waits for the last battle callout to finish speaking | a fixed delay per side as observed (1.32 s Autobot, 3.52 s Decepticon) | timing |
| Speech-driven deff lengths | the side mode's super and hit deffs, deff 144 and the Megatron jackpots run as long as their speech | the captured length (the side mode's total can come 0.5 s early or late) | timing |
| Allspark warning refused | the eject warning 0x157 is refused while a higher priority sample holds its channel, then the ball is ejected at once | modelled from the sample masks and the call flags' low byte (inferred); the deffs' own speech is not always the ROM's, so a few ejects still warn where the ROM does not (megatron_decepticon 39.2 s, wizard_multiball 47.1 s) | inferred |
| Multiball end with a ball in the Megatron lock | the ROM counts the multiball over about 2 s after the last drain when a ball waits in the lock | over at the drain (optimus_autobot: the total 1.9 s early) | timing |
| Locked balls during another multiball | the lock device keeps no ball while a multiball or the side mode runs [0x0100a1f8] | the locked balls are kicked out at the multiball release spacing (0.13 / 0.61 / 0.93 s), with no sound or leff; no trace covers it | inferred |
| Super pops and super spinner | [0x0102c8c8] [0x010320a4] | as the code: super value and hit count per lighting, deffs 150 / 147 with the hits left and the burst total, 151 / 148 at the end; no trace plays them (the lit state is kept per player, the ROM keeps it in game flags 0x4d / 0x4c) | code only |
| Rule leff restarts | the lamp rules restart a mode's rule leff when another leff ends (e.g. 177, 71, 47 twice) | started once | interim |
