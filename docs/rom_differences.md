# Departures from the ROM

Every place the game differs from tf_180, why, and what replaces it. Interim rows go away when the ROM
extraction delivers the data.

| Area | ROM | Here | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| Score display (deff 19) | 0x0102fb8c | drawn live from the captured draw calls; the dim level of players not up and the font for scores wider than 87 dots are inferred (tf/score_screen.py) | inferred details |
| Display effects with values | the ROM prints live values (scores, counts, credits, high scores) | the printf texts of 72 effects are cleared from the captured frames and drawn live from their ROM format (scripts/gen_media.py value_texts), fit-font texts in the font the ROM's fit picks (deff_values.gd), with the values the rules pass (pop: 3,170; bonus: count value and total; which RAM value each text prints is inferred); a slot with no value keeps the capture's string, and texts in moving or fading frames stay as captured (the bonus border frames), as do the pages a capture does not hold (bonus 2X and up); the status panel is live | interim, until A gives each text's argument source |
| Deffs without frames (39) | they draw live state or nothing in the capture window | nothing drawn (the previous screen stays) | interim |
| Scoring and rules | switch handlers and the features they call | as the ROM for the handlers, slings, pops, lanes, spinner, Bumblebee, 2-bank, combos, shot multipliers, skill shots, Energon, the Allspark award, mode-start shots and the eight battles (rom/rules/modes/*.md; tf/features/). The Optimus figure (motor coil 30 to its 7.65 s time limit, the hit kicker coil 12) and the orbit gate (coil 5, 1.48 s on each counted left orbit) follow the traces; the combo arrows (leff 34) are not drawn, as in the ROM (1.80 never runs that leff); the roving 3X follows the code (not traced). Inferred: the left orbit token's length (2.26 s, observed between 2.18 and 2.35 s), double scoring's end split, the right orbit ignore windows (3 s after a left orbit pass, the center lane, a plunge or the back door), the battle hit deffs' lengths | interim, until the specs |
| Battle and Energon deffs without frames | deffs 94, 96, 111, 118, 119, 124, 125 have no capture | lengths, leffs and sounds from the traces (tf/features/battles.py UNCAPTURED, energon.py); nothing drawn | inferred |
| Battle hit deff sounds | each hit deff picks its callouts by hit index and chains follow-ups (e.g. Blackout 0x1c3 then 0x1c4, [0x0101db34]) | the hit sound with its index; the chained follow-ups are not played | interim |
| Multiball and wizard deffs without frames | deffs 59, 60, 62 (Optimus Autobot), 79, 139 (Megatron), 88 (wizard multiball) have no capture | lengths, leffs and sounds from the traces (UNCAPTURED in tf/features/optimus.py, megatron.py, wizard.py); nothing drawn | inferred |

| Allspark warning refused | the eject warning 0x157 is refused while a higher priority sample holds its channel, then the ball is ejected at once | modelled from the sample masks and the call flags' low byte (inferred); the deffs' own speech is not always the ROM's, so 10 of the traces' 37 ejects come 0.85 s early or late (allspark_energon 45.6 s, battle_blackout 26.9 / 42.1 s, battle_bumblebee 27.1 s, battle_devastator 29.3 s, megatron_decepticon 39.3 s, switches 144.5 s, wizard_all_hail_megatron 60.3 s, wizard_multiball 28.4 / 47.1 s) | inferred |
| Locked balls during another multiball | the lock device keeps no ball while a multiball or the side mode runs [0x0100a1f8] | the locked balls are kicked out at the multiball release spacing (0.13 / 0.61 / 0.93 s), with no sound or leff; no trace covers it | inferred |
| Megatron lock at the end of a game | the device keeps its balls [0x0100a1f8]: the next game finds them there | the kept balls are kicked out and go home when the game ends (hardware.yaml `hold_megatron`): MPF starts a game with every ball in the trough. During a game they stay over balls and players as in the ROM | MPF limit |
| A kept ball kicked by the ball search | the search pulses the lock's coil; the ROM counts its balls from the devices [0x0103a5ec], so the ball is in play again | the ball is counted in play again and the player's lock counts stay (tf/features/megatron.py `_left_check`); no trace covers it | inferred |
| Multiball launches | the multiball task launches at most the balls the trough holds [0x0103a87c] | the same cap at the request (tf/os_layer.py `multiball_start`); the ROM's kick-out of other devices' balls for the missing ones (device list 0x040d18b8, param_2 = 1) is not modelled | code, partly |
| ADD-A-BALL | [0x01024a60]: multiball_start(balls in play + 1, save 312, grace 187), the flag only when it started | as the code; the ball the Allspark holds is added to the count here because MPF counts it in play. During a multiball's ball save the ROM's count is the requested one: that case is inferred | code, partly inferred |
| Game restart (adj 36) | START held on ball 2+ runs game_init at once [0x0001a6fc]: ball 1 is served while the old ball is still on the playfield | the game ends at once, the new one serves its first ball when the old ball has drained (MPF starts a ball on an empty playfield) | MPF limit |
| Extra ball and special | lit by [0x01007b40] / [0x01008528], collected at the right orbit [0x01033488] | as the code (tf/features/awards.py); no trace plays them | code only |
| Adjustments not modelled | adj 90-95, the combo champion tables | not read: no effect (tests/bench/test_adjustments.py `NOT_MODELLED`) | interim |

Showing deff 39 after a credit in attract was tried and contradicts all 24 reference traces (they insert coins in attract with the default adj 46 and the ROM does not show it), so the trigger is not the credit; the task body (LAB_00017f28) is not decompiled. Needs a ROM probe (tools/emu) of what calls [0x00017fa0] | interim |
| Adjustments 84 and 85 | the orbit gate and the Optimus motor are "disabled by adjustment" [0x0102c0cc] [0x00017120] | the gate coil and the motor are not driven (and the ball search skips the gate); what else the ROM skips is not traced | code, partly inferred |
| Super pops and super spinner | [0x0102c8c8] [0x010320a4] | as the code: super value and hit count per lighting, deffs 150 / 147 with the hits left and the burst total, 151 / 148 at the end; no trace plays them (the lit state is kept per player, the ROM keeps it in game flags 0x4d / 0x4c) | code only |
